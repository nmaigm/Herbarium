Agrostis stolonifera L.
Sp. Pl., ed. 1, 62 (1753).

Synonyms:
Agrostis alba, auct. non L.

Morphology:
Stoloniferous perennial without rhizomes, to 0.65(1.2) m; panicle 2-20(32) cm, open at anthesis, contracted before and after, the spikelets condensed about the branches and branchlets, the lower branches often naked below; spikelets 1.8-3 mm; glumes s. bequal or unequal, acute; lemma 1.3-2.1 mm, glabrous, the outer nerves sometimes excurrent; palea well developed, 3/5-3/4 the length of the lemma; awn to 3 mm long, but variał le and often quite absent.
