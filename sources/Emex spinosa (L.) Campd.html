Emex spinosa (L.) Campd.
Monogr. Rumex 58, t. 1 (1819).

Synonyms:
Rumex spinosus L., Sp. Pl., ed. 1, 337 (1753).

Morphology:
Annual herb to 40 cm, stems decumbent or erect; leaves 2-10 x 1-6 cm, ovate to deltoid; petiole 2-10 cm, male flowers 1.5 mm, pedicellate; female flowers 5-6 mm, sessile; fruiting perianth 4-5 x 2.5-4 mm including the spines, pitted, with recurved spines.
