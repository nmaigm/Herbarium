Forsskaolea tenacissima L.
Oppobalsam. Decl. 18 (1764).

Synonyms:
Caidbeja adhaerens Forssk., Fl. Aegypt.-Arab. 82 (1775).
Forsskaolea cossoniana Webb, Otia Hisp. 49, t. 45 (1839).

Morphology:
Perennial herb to 80 cm, often woody at the base; stems erect or ascending; leaves 1-4 x 0.5-3 cm, broadly obovate, serrate, green with hooked hairs on the upper surface, densely white-pubescent on the lower; petiole 1-2 cm; stipules scarious, persistent; flowers 1-4 in axillary clusters, on subsessile receptacles, surrounded by 3-6 bracts; bracts 5-8 x 1-2 mm, spathulate, acute, densely white-silky; perianth of male flowers green-hispid; ovary 1-few in each inflorescence.
