Vicia narbonensis L.
Sp. Pl., ed. 1, 737 (1753).

Morphology:
Annual herb, 15-40 cm, glabrescent to sparsely hairy; stems erect, robust, thick, angular, branched; stipules 1-1.5 cm, suborbicular, dentate to incised; leaves 5-10 cm; rhachis 1-3 mm; tendrils branched; leaflets 4-6, 2-5 x 1-3 cm, subsessile, elliptical to ovate of lanceolate, the apex obtuse-mucronate, the margins entire, dentate or serrate; peduncles 1-2 mm, thick; racemes 3-5-flowered; calyx 0.8-1.2 cm, the tube ± as long as the unequal teeth; corolla 2-3 cm, purplish-violet; pod 3.5-5 x 1-1.2 cm, flattened, oblong-linear, slightly curved, beaked, tuberculate-dentate at the margins; seeds 4-5 mm, subglobose, dark brown.
