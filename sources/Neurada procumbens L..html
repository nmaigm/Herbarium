Neurada procumbens L.
Sp. Pl., ed. 1, 441 (1753).

Morphology:
Annual herb 10-40 cm, densely grey-tomentose; stems prostrate; leaves 0.5-3 x 0.3-1.5 cm, oblong-ovate, sinuate-pinnatifid; sepals 2-2.5 x 1-1.5 mm, ovate-triangular, acute; petals 1 x1 mm, white, cream or pinkish; fruit 1-2.2 mm diam., with 2-8 mm spines.
