Zygophyllum simplex L.
Mant. 68 (1767).

Synonyms:
Zygophyllum portulacoides Forssk., Fl. Aegypt.-Arab. 88 (1775), Icones t. 12 В (1776).

Morphology:
Glabrous annual or short-lived perennial, 10-40 cm; stems prostrate, much-branched; leaves 0.5-2.5 cm, simple, sessile, fleshy, cylindrical to obovoid, obtuse; stipules 1-2 mm, membranous, lanceolate, acute; pedicel 1-2 mm, elongate in fruit; flowers axillary, 1 or 2; sepals 1.5-2 mm, obovate, caducous, the margin scarious; petals 3 mm, yellow, long-clawed; capsule 2-3 mm, obovoid, glabrous, the lobes narrowly winged; style 2-3 mm, persistent; seeds 1 mm, broadly elliptic, smooth.
