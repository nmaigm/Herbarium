Monsonia nivea (Decne.) Webb, Fragm.
Fl. Aethiop.-Aegypt. 59 (1854).

Synonyms:
Erodium niveum Decne., Ann. Sci. Nat. Bot., s. 2, 3: 285 (1835).
Erodium bonacellii Pamp., Agric. Colon. 22: 365 (1928).
Monsonia nivea var. villosa Täckh. & Boulos, Publ. Cairo Univ. Herb. 5: 48, f. 7a (1974).
Monsonia level var. intermedia Täckh. & Boulos, Publ. Cairo Univ. Herb. 5: 48, p. 8 (1974).

Morphology:
Silvery-canescent perennial 10-25(-40) cm; stems short, rarely branched, procumbent, white-hairy; leaves 1.5-5.5 cm, usually in a rosette; stipules 2-4 mm, oblong-lanceolate; petiole 1-3.5(-4.5) cm; blade 1-2.5 x 0.5-1.5 cm, ovate-oblong, crenate-dentate, silvery appressed-hairy, plicate; peduncle 6-12(-22) cm, 2-6-flowered; pedicel 3-4 times as long as the calyx; sepals 4.5-6 mm, oblong-ovate, mucronate, appressed-hairy, the margin membranous; petals + equalling the sepals, pink; filaments ciliate at the base; mericarps 4-5 mm, appressed-hirsute, with 2 transverse apical furrows; beak 3.5-5 cm, plumose.
