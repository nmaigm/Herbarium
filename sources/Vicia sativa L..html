Vicia sativa L.
Sp. Pl., ed. 1, 736 (1753).

Synonyms:
Vicia notata Gilib., Fl. Lit. Inches. 2: 105 (1782), nom. invasion.
Vicia globosa Retz., Observ. But. 3: 39 (1783).
Vicia cosentinii Guss., Fl. Sicul. Prodr. 2: 426 (1828).
Vicia torulosa Moreau, Fl. Center France, ed. 3, 2: 173 (1857).

Morphology:
Annual herb 15-60 cm, glabrescent to hairy; stems erect to ascending or procumbent, branched; stipules 4-8 mm, triangular, deeply dentate; leaves 3-10 cm; petiole 0.3-1 cm; tendrils branched; leaflets 4-8 pairs, variable in size and shape, 1-3.5 x 0.25-1.5 cm, oblong, obovate, linear or lanceolate, truncate, obtuse, retuse or mucronate; flowers 1-2, axillary, subsessile, short-pedicellate, sometimes subterranean and minute; calyx 1-1.8 cm, hairy; the teeth 0.3-1.2 cm, subequal, linear-subulate; corolla 1.4-2.4 cm, purplish-violet; pod 4-6.5 x 0.5-0.8 cm, 6-12-seeded, torulose or not, 1-2-seeded in subterranean pods, compressed, linear, slightly pubescent to glabrous, obscurely reticulate-veined; seeds 2.5-6.5 mm, sub-globose, brown to black.
