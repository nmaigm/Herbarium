Scrophularia deserti Delile
Descr. Egypte, Hist. Nat. 240, t. 33, f. 1 (1814).

Synonyms:
Scrophularia marginata Boiss., Diagn. Pl. Orient., ser. 1, 4: 72 (1844).

Morphology:
Glabrous perennial 20-60 cm, woody at the base; stems numerous from the base: leaves 1-3(-4) x 0.8-1.5 cm, opposite, fleshy, obtusely dentate to shallowly lobed, white-margined; petiole 1-2.5 cm; lower leaves spathulate, petiolate, the upper short-petiolate or sessile, dentate; cymes many-flowered; peduncle 0.5-1.5 cm, bracts and bracteoles minute, scale-like; pedicel 0.5-1 mm; calyx-lobes 1.5-2 mm, white-margined: corolla 4-5 mm, deep red; staminode white, ovate; capsule 3-3.5 mm, subglobose, mucronate.
