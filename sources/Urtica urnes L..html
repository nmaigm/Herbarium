Urtica urens L.
Sp. Pl., ed. 1, 984 (1753).

Morphology:
Monoecious annual to 60 cm, stems erect; leaves 1.5-6 x 1-3.5 cm, ovate, coarsely dentate; petiole 1-2.5 cm, stipules 4 at each node; panicles clustered in the leaf axils, shorter than the petiole, with numerous female and few male flowers, perianth segments (tepals) of female flowers with ciliate margins; achene 1.5-2 mm.
