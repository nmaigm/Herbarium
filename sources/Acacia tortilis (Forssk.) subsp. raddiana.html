Acacia tortilis (Forssk.) .subsp. raddiana
Hayne, Getreue Darstell Gew. 10, t. 31 (1827).

Synonyms:
Mimosa tortilis Forssk., Fl. Aegypt.-Arab. 176 (1775).
Acacia raddiana Savi, Alc. Acacie Egiz. 1 (1830).

Morphology:
Tree 5-12 m; not branched from the base, with a distinct trunk and irregular crown; juvenile branches, stipular spines in pairs, 2-5 mm, hooked, mixed with straight spines to 10 cm; pinnae 3-10 pairs; leaflets 6-20 pairs, with a small gland under each pair, 0.5-5 x 0.5-1 mm, oblong, obtuse, glabrous or appressed-hairy; peduncles 0.5-3.5 cm, with involucel below the middle; heads axillary; calyx 1-2 mm; corolla 1.5-2.5 mm; whitish-yellow; pod 3-8 x 0.6-1.2 cm, contorted or spirally coiled, constricted between the seeds, longitudinally veined, glabrous or tomentose; seeds 4-7 x 4-6 mm, compressed, the areole 3-5 x 2-3 mm, brown, smooth.
