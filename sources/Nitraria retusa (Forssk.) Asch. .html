Nitraria retusa (Forssk.) Asch.
Verh. Bot. Vereins. Prov. Brandenb. 18: 94 (1876).

Synonyms:
Peganum retusum Forssk., Fl. Aegypt.-Arab. LXVI, 211 (1775).
Nitraria senegalensis Poir. in Lam., Encycl. 4: 493 (1797),
Nitraria tridentata Desf., Fl. Atlant. 1: 372 (1798).
Nitraria sericea Jaub. & Spach, III. PI. Orient. 3: 142, t. 294 (1850).

Morphology:
Shrub 1-2 m; stems many, spinescent, appressed-canescent; leaves 1-2.5 x 0.5-1.5 cm, alternate or in fascicles, petiolate, fleshy, obovate-cuneate, retuse or crenate-dentate at the apex; stipules minute, persistent; flowers 5-8 mm, in loose dichotomous cymes on young branches; pedicel 3-6 mm; sepals 2-2.5 mm, persistent; petals 4-5 mm, greenish-white, hispid; stamens 15; fruit a fleshy drupe, 0.6-1 x 0.5-0.6 cm, trigonous, pear-shaped, red.
