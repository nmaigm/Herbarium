Silybum marianum (L.) Gaertn. var marianum
Fruct. Sem. Pl. 2: 378 (1791).

Synonyms:
Carduus marianus L., Sp. Pl., ed. 1, 823 (1753).

Morphology:
Glabrous biennial 0.4-1.5 m; stems erect, usually unbranched, sulcate; leaves to 50 x 25 cm, oblanceolate, sinuate-lobed, white-blotched, the margins spinose; basal leaves large in a rosette; the cauline smaller, sessile, shortly decurrent, with spinose basal auricles inflorescence branched; capitula solitary; phyllaries imbricate, glabrous, with a broadly oblong base 1-1.5 x 0.8-1 cm, the apex pectinate-spinose, with a spine 3-7 cm; corollas papillose, connate into a tube c. 1.5 cm; anthers c. 6 mm, linear, with an acute apical purple ; corolla-tube c. 2 cm, slender; corolla-lobes 6-7 mm; filaments glandular-appendage; style c. 3 cm, branches united almost to the apex: achenes 6-7 x 3 mm. oblong compressed; pappus 1.2-1.5 cm, of rigid white barbellate bristles, connate at the base, deciduous.
