Rostraria cristata (L.) Tzvelev
Novosti Sist. Vyssh. Rast. 7: 47 (1971).

Synonyms:
Festuca cristata L., Sp. Pl., ed. 1, 76 (1753), not Aira cristata L., nec Koeleria cristata Pers.
Festuca gerardii Vill., Hist. Pl. Dauphiné 1: 249 (1786).
Festuca phleoides Vill., Hist. Pl. Dauphiné 1: 249 (1786), nom superfl., based on Festuca gerardii Vill.
Koeleria phleoides (Vill.) Pers., Syn. Pl. 1: 97 (1805).
Lophochloa phleoides (Vill.) Rchb., Fl. Germ. Excurs. 42 (1830).
Lophochloa cristata (L.) Hyl., Bot. Notiser 1953: 355 (1953).
Coeleria gerardii (Vill.) Shinners, Rhodora 58:95 (1956), not Coeleria gerardii Munro ex Benth. & Hook. (1847).

Morphology:
Culms to 60 cm; panicle 1-12 cm, cylindrical and dense or pyramidal and lax and ± lobed; spikelets 3-7.5 mm, 3 to 6(10)-flowered; rhachilla not produced, subglabrous or with hairs less than 0.5 mm; glumes unequal, glabrous or hairy, acute, the lower 2-3 mm, 1-nerved, narrowly lanceolate, the upper 2.5-3.5 mm, 3-nerved, elliptic; lemmas 2.5-3.5 mm, glabrous or sparsely hairy, the surface smooth or minutely papillose, acute at the tip; awn 1-3 mm, terminal or almost so.
