Anabasis setifera Moq.
Chenop. Monogr. Enum. 164 (1840).

Morphology:
Subshrub 30-60 cm, glabrous; stems richly branched, the old branches woody with split bark, the lateral branches fleshy, 4-angled, papillose; leaves 0.5-1.5 cm, cylindrical or clavate, usually spreading, with a terminal caducous bristle; flowers 3-7, in axillary clusters on the upper parts of the stems; perianth-segments 1.2 mm; stamens 1 mm; staminodes minute; ovary papillose; fruiting perianth 5 mm diam. including the wings; wings 5, unequal, membranous, yellowish-white; seeds vertical.
