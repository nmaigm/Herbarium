Phalaris paradoxa L.
Sp. Pl., ed. 2, 1665 (1763).

Synonyms:
Phalaris praemorsa Lam, Fl. Franç. 3: 566 (1778).
Phalaris appendiculata Schult., Mant. 2: 216 (1824).
Phalaris paradoxa L. var. praemorsa (Lam.) Coss. & Durieu, Expl. Sci. Algeria 2:25 (1854).

Morphology:
Annual to 1 m; panicle to 9 x 2 cm, dense, oblong in outline but tapering to a narrow base; spikelets falling from the inflorescence in clusters of 6 or 7, with 5 or 6 sterile, reduced or ± deformed spikelets forming an involucre about a single (rarely 2) fertile spikelet; glumes of fertile spikelet 5.5-8.2 mm, acuminate to subulate, winged above, the wing with a tooth-like projection near the middle; sterile florets of fertile spikelet obsolete; fertile floret 2.5-3.3 mm, glabrous or with a few short hairs near the tip.
