Morphology:
the pomegranate tree (Punica granatum) is a deciduous shrub or small tree, typically 6-10 meters tall, with spiny branches, simple and opposite evergreen leaves (2-10 cm long), and red flowers clustered at branch ends. The hexagonal fruit has a hard, leathery rind that is red when ripe, and its interior contains numerous arils—the juicy, edible parts surrounding seeds—which are separated by a spongy white tissue.
