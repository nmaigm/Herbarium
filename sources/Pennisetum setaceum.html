Pennisetum setaceum (Forssk.) Chiov.
Boll. Soc. But. Ital. 1923: 113 (1923).

Synonyms:
Phalaris setacea Forssk., Fl. Aegypt.-Arab. 17 (1775).
Pennisetum macrostachyum Fresen., Mus. Senckenberg. 2: 135 (1837), non (Brongn.) Trin. (1834).
Pennisetum ruppellii Steud., Nomencl. Bot., ed. 2, 2: 298 (1841), based on Pennisetum macrostachyum Fresen.
Pennisetum spectabile Fig. & De Not., Mem. Reale Accad. Sci. Turin, ser. 2, 12: 248 (1852).
Pennisetum tiberiadis Boiss., Diagn. Pl. Orient. 2(13): 43 (1854).
Pennisetum asperifolium, auct. non (Desf.) Kunth

Morphology:
Densely tufted perennial to 1.3 m, the culms usually unbranched; ligule a line of hairs; leaf-blades inrolled with noticeably thickened midrib on the upper surface, rigid, harsh, glaucous; panicle 6-30 cm, linear; rhachis with shallowly angular ribs below the stumpless peduncle-scars, glabrous to pilose; involucre enclosing 1 sessile and 0-1(2)- pedicellate spikelets, borne upon a slender pubescent stipe 1-3 mm; bristles loosely plumose, the longest 1.6-4 cm; spikelets (4.5)5-7 mm, lanceolate; lower glume to 2 mm, or vestigial; upper glume 1/5-2/3 the length of the spikelet; lower floret male or barren, the lemma as long as the spikelet; upper lemma similar to the lower; anther-tips glabrous.
