Fagonia taeckholmiana Hadidi
Candollea 21: 29, Γ. 7Α (1966).

Morphology:
Almost glabrous perennial with woody base, 10-25 cm; stems procumbent or ascending, terete, striate; stipular spines absent or minute; leaves 1-foliolate, the lowermost ? 3-foliolate; petiole 1-3 mm, with a distinct joint between the petiole and the blade; leaflets 4-6 mm, lanceolate, mucronate, fleshy; pedicel 2-3 mm; flowers 1-1.2 cm diam. at anthesis; sepals 3.5 x 1.5 mm, ovate, glandular, deciduous; petals 5-6 mm, pink; capsule 4 x 5 mm; style 1-1.5 mm.
