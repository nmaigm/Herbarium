Orobanche ramosa L.
Sp. Pl., ed. 1, 633 (1753).

Synonyms:
Phelipaea ramosa (L.) C. A. Mey., Collection Pfl. Casp. Meer 104 (1831).
Phelipanche ramosa (L.) Pomel, Nouv. Mat. Fl. Atlant. 1: 103 (1874).
Kopsia ramosa (L.) Dumort., Comment. Bot. 16 (1822).

Morphology:
Annual or short-lived perennial, 10-40 cm; stems erect, usually much-branched, swollen at the base, glandular-hairy; inflorescence of loosely branched spikes; flowers 1-1.5(-1.7) cm, subtended by 1 bract 5-8 mm, ovate-lanceolate, and 2 bracteoles 4-7 mm, linear-lanceolate; calyx 5-8 mm, 4-toothed; calyx-teeth 2-3 mm, triangular, acuminate; calyx-tube 3-5 mm; corolla 1.2-1.5(-1.8) cm, 2-lipped, glandular-hairy, ± straight, slightly inflated at the base, narrowly funnel-shaped above, cream-yellowish, whitish, bluish or violet-blue; lobes of the lower corolla-lip rounded, denticulate to ± entire; filaments glabrous or hairy at the base; capsule 5-6 mm.
