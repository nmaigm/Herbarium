Euphorbia hirta L.
Sp. Pl., ed. 1, 454 (1753).

Synonyms:
Euphorbia pilulifera, sensu Boiss. in DC., Prodr. 15(2): 21 (1862), not L. (1753).
Chamaesyce hirta (L.) Millsp., Publ. Field Columb. Mus., Bot. 2: 303 (1909).

Morphology:
Hispid annual 20-50 cm; hairs multicellular, drying yellow; stems erect to ascending, branched mainly from the base; leaves 1-4 x 0.5-1.5 cm, opposite, rhombic-ovate to -lanceolate, asymmetrically cuneate at the base, acute, the margin sharply serrate; stipules 1-2 mm, subulate; cyathia in dense axillary clusters 0.5-1 cm diam.; peduncle 3-8 mm, capsule c. 1.2 x 1.2 mm, sharply trigonous, appressed-hirsute; seeds 0.8 mm, tetragonous, transversely wrinkled.
