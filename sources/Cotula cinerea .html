Cotula cinerea Delile, Descr.
Egypte, Hist. Nat. 275, t. 47, f. 4 (1814).

Synonyms:
Brocchia cinerea (Delile) Vis., Pl. Aegypt. Nub. 35 (1836).
Tanacetum cinereum (Delile) DC., Prodr. 6: 131 (1838).

Morphology:
Densely grey-canescent annual, 10-25 cm; stems many from the base, branched, leafy. ascending: leaves 0.3-1.2 x 0.1-0.6 cm, oblong in outline and 4-6-lobed, or 3-lobed at the apex, the lobes oblong-elliptic, entire, subacute; uppermost leaves smaller undivided, oblong-linear; peduncle 1-5 cm, ebracteate; capitula discoid, 6-8 mm diam., subglobose; involucre 2.5-3.5 mm; phyllaries 2-seriate, imbricate, densely woolly, scarious towards the apex; receptacle conical, epaleate; florets c. 2 mm, 4-lobed, yellow; achenes 1-1.2 x 0.4-0.6 mm, oblong-oblanceolate, sparsely glandular-punctate, striate; pappus absent.
