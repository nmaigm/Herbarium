Tamarix nilotica (Ehrenb.) Bunge, Tent.
Tamaric. 54 (1852).

Synonyms:
Tamarix (gallica) nilotica Ehrenb., Linnaea 2: 269 (1827).
Tamarix arabica Bunge, Tent. Tamaric. 55 (1852).
Tamarix mannifera Bunge, Tent. Tamaric. 63 (1852).
Tamarix arborea (Ehenb.) Bunge, Tent. Tamaric. 67 (1852).

For detailed taxonomy and nomenclature see Zohary, Fl. Palaest. 2: 355-358 (1972).

Morphology:
Shrub or tree 2-5 m; bark reddish-brown; young parts glabrous or papillose; leaves 1-3 mm, sessile, ovate-deltoid to lanceolate, slightly clasping, acute to acuminate; racemes 5 - 10 * 0.3 cm, lateral, slender, sometimes paniculate; bracts 1-2 mm, narrowly triangular, acuminate; pedicel 0.5-0.75 mm; flowers 3-4 mm diam.; sepals 5, 1-1.2 mm, margin membranous, apex obtuse; petals 5, 1.5-2 mm, ovate-elliptic, white, caducous; stamens 4-5; filaments exserted, inserted in the sinuses of the disc; styles 3, club-shaped; capsule 3.5 - 4.5 * 2 mm, pyramidal; seeds 0.5 mm, terete, with an apical tuft of hairs 1.5-2 mm.
