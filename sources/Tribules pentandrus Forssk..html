Tribulus pentandrus Forssk.
Fl. Aegypt.-Arab. 88 (1775).

Synonyms:
Tribulus alatus Delile, Descr. Egypte, Hist. Nat. 62 (1814), nom. superfl.
Tribulus longipetalus Viv., Plant. Aegypt. Dec. 4: 10 (1830).
Tribulus alatus Delile var. odontopteris Kralik, Ann. Sci. Nat., sér. 3, 11: 30 (1849), nom. illeg.

Morphology:
Pubescent annual or short-lived perennial; stems 20-60 cm, prostrate; leaves 2-6 cm; leaflets 3-7 pairs, 0.3-1.2 x 0.2-0.4 cm, ovate-lanceolate; stipules 3-6 mm; flowers 6-9 mm diam. at anthesis; pedicel 3-6 mm, elongate in fruit to 1.2 cm; sepals 3.5-4.5 mm, caducous; petals 4-6 mm, yellow or white; filaments 2-3.5 mm; fruit pyramidal to spherical, 0.6-1.2 cm diam. including the wings; mericarps 2-6 mm diam., sparsely hairy, 2-winged; wings triangular, entire or dentate-serrate.
