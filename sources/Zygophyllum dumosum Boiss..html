Zygophyllum dumosum Boiss.
Diagn. Pl. Orient., ser. 1, 8: 125 (1849).

Morphology:
Appressed-canescent low shrub, 30-80 cm; stems many, intricate, angular; old stems woody, with fissured bark; young branches herbaceous, canescent; leaves 2-foliolate; stipules 1-1.5 mm, broadly triangular, connate at the base; petiole 0.5-1 cm, cylindrical, fleshy, persistent; leaflets 0.5-1 cm, canescent, cylindrical, deciduous; flowers solitary, axillary; pedicel 0.5-1 cm, elongate in fruit; sepals 6-8 mm oblong-ovate, the margin scarious; petals 1.2-1.6 cm, white, spathulate; filaments 6-8 mm, the basal scales suborbicular, with dentate apex; capsule 0.8 x 1 cm, conspicuously winged, the wings broader than the capsule; style 2-3 mm; seeds 3-3.5 x 2 mm, rhomboid, brownish.
