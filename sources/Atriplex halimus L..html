Atriplex halimus L.
Sp. Pl., ed. 1, 1052 (1753).

Morphology:
Shrub 0.5-1.2 m, monoecious; stems woody at the base, much branched, whitish; leaves 1.5-4.5 х 0.5-3.5 cm, sessile or short-petiolate, ovate-rhombic or ovate-triangular, tapered at both ends, entire or sharply lobed, silvery-white; flowers in dense, almost leafless, spicate, terminal panicles; fruiting bracteoles 2.5-4 x 2.5-4.5 mm, reniform to broadly triangular-ovate, connate at the base, the free upper part slightly dentate; seeds 1-2 mm, lenticular, dark brown.
