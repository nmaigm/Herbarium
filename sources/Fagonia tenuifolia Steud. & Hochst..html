Fagonia tenuifolia Steud. & Hochst.
ex Boiss., Fl. Orient. 1: 909 (1867).

Synonyms:
Fagonia bischarorum Schweinf., Bull. Herb. Boissier 7, App. 2: 276 (1899).
Fagonia flamandii Batt., Bull. Soc. Bot. France 47: 248 (1900).

Morphology:
Glabrous low shrub 15-25 cm; stems erect, terete, sulcate; internodes 2-4 cm; stipular spines 2-5 mm; leaves 3-foliolate; petiole 0.8-1.6 cm; leaflets 0.6-1.6 x 0.2 cm, linear, the terminal leaflet longer than the 2 lateral; pedicel 1-1.5 cm, elongate in fruit, 2-3 times as long as the capsule; flowers 1.2-1.5 cm diam. at anthesis; sepals 3-3.5 x 1.5-2 mm, ovate, glabrous, caducous; petals 6-8 mm, rose-pink; capsule 5 x 5 mm, hairy; style 2-3 mm.
