Euphorbia retusa Forssk.
Fl. Aegypt.-Arab. 93 (1775).

Synonyms:
Euphorbia kahirensis Raeusch., Nomencl. Bot., ed. 3, 140 (1797), nom. illeg.
Euphorbia cornuta Pers., Syn. Pl. 2: 17 (1806), nom. illeg.

Morphology:
Glabrous glaucous perennial 20-60 cm, sometimes flowering the first year; stems erect, many from a woody base; leaves sessile, the cauline 1-3.5(-5) x 0.3-0.6 cm, oblong-linear, alternate; base rounded, apex acute to retuse, margin acutely serrate; umbellar and floral leaves opposite, broadest at the base, sharply serrate-denticulate; cyathia 2.5-3 mm, in forked umbels, pedunculate; involucre campanulate, lobes triangular, fringed; glands with 2-fid horns; capsule 5-6 x 4 mm, conical-ovoid, 3-lobed, smooth; seeds 2.5 mm; caruncle yellowish, about 1/2 as long as the seed, smooth.
