Indigofera arabica Jaub. & Spach
III. Pl. Orient. 5: 89 (1856).

Morphology:
Perennial herb woody at the base, 15-30 cm, silvery-strigose; stems branched, especially at the base; stipules 2-2.5 mm, subulate; petiole 0.5-1 cm; leaves 3-5-foliolate; leaflets 0.5-1.5 x 0.2-0.6 cm, opposite, obovate or oblanceolate, downcurved at the rounded, truncate or retuse apex; peduncle 0 or very short; racemes shorter than the subtending leaf, 5-12-flowered, dense; calyx 3-3.5 mm, strigose, deeply divided into subulate teeth; standard strigose on the dorsal surface; pod 0.5-1.1 x 0.3 cm, flattened, silvery-strigose, the style base persistent on the obtuse-upcurved apex, 2-5-seeded; seeds 1.5 x 1.5 mm, rounded-rectangular, with thick margins.
