Argemone mexicana L.
Sp. Pl., ed. 1, 508 (1753).

Morphology:
Annual herb 20-80 cm, with yellow latex; stems erect, branched; leaves 5-10 x 2.5-5 cm; rather fleshy, oblong-elliptic, pinnatifid; the margins and veins on the lower surface armed with sharp prickles; flowers axillary, sessile; petals 2-3.5 x 2-2.5 cm, yellow, obovate; capsule 3-4 x 1.5-2 cm, oblong, armed with prickles, the lid covered by the stigmatic disc.
