Astragalus sieberi DC.
Prodr. 2: 295 (1835).

Morphology:
Subshrub 20-40 cm, appressed white-hairy; stems many from the base; stipules c. 8 mm, triangular, acuminate, ciliate, persistent in fruit; leaves 8-20 cm, petiole and rhachis 2 mm broad, narrower towards the apex, becoming spiny and persistent; leaflets 20-25 pairs, 2-5 x 2 mm, ovate-oblong, appressed-hairy on the lower surface, almost glabrous on the upper; racemes few-flowered. mainly at the base of the branches; flowers short-pedicellate; calyx c. 1.2 cm, the teeth 3 mm, ciliate; corolla 2.4-2.6 cm, yellow; pod 3-4.5(-5), 0.6-0.8 cm including the sharp beak, straight or slightly curved, woody, reticulate-veined, almost glabrous; seeds 4-5 mm, quadrangular, compressed, brown.
