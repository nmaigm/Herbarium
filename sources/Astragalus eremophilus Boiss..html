Astragalus eremophilus Boiss.
Diagn. Pl. Orient. 2: 54 (1843).

Synonyms:
Astragalus falcinellus Boiss., Diagn. Pl. Orient. 9: 63 (1849).

Morphology:
Annual 15-25 cm, white-villous throughout; stems ascending or prostrate, branched from the base; stipules 2-3 mm, triangular; leaves 2.5-4 cm; leaflets 6-12, short-petioliolate, 0.4-1.2 x 0.2-0.5 cm, broadly elliptic to obovate, obtuse, truncate or retuse at the apex, white-villous on both surfaces; peduncles 1-4 cm; racemes 1-5-flowered; bracts 1.5-2 mm; calyx 3-5 mm, white-villous, the teeth longer than the tube; corolla 5-6 mm, pale yellow; pod 1.5-2.5 x 0.25-0.3 cm, 8-10 times as long as broad, strongly falcate, linear in outline, attenuate towards the apex, white-villose.
