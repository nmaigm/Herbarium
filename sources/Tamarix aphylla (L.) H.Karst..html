Tamarix aphylla (L.) H. Karst.
Deutschl. Fl. Pharm.-Med. Bot. 641 (1882).

Synonyms:
Thuja aphylla L., Cent. Pl. 1: 35 (1755), pro parte
Tamarix orientalis Forssk., Fl. Aegypt.-Arab. 206 (1775).
Tamarix articulata Vahl, Symb. Bot. 2: 48, t. 32 (1791), nom. illeg.

Morphology:
Glabrous tree or large shrub, 3-12 m; bark reddish-brown; stems with tender green shoots; branchlets jointed, green; leaves sheath-like, with a short pointed apex, without a blade; inflorescence paniculate, the racemes 3-8 x 0.3-0.4 cm; bracts I mm, triangular, clasping, acuminate; pedicel 0.5-1 mm; flowers 2-2.5 mm diam., sepals 5, 1-1.5 mm; petals 5, 1.5-2 mm, white or pinkish; stamens 5, the filaments jointed with the deep sinuses of the disc; styles 3; capsule 3-4 mm, pyriform; seeds 0.5 mm, with an apical tuft of hairs.
