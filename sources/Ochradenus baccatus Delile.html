Ochradenus baccatus Delile
Descr. Egypte, Hist. Nat. 63 (1814).

Morphology:
Dioecious shrub 1-2.5 m, glabrous throughout; young branches slender, old branches spinescent; leaves 1-4 cm, linear; racemes many-flowered; pedicels 1-2 mm; flowers yellow; sepals 1-1.5 mm, oblong-ovate; petals usually absent; disc reflexed; stamens 10-18; berry 3-5.5 mm, globose, subsessile, white at maturity, drying red; seeds 1.5-1.8 x 1-1.5 mm, tuberculate, dark brown.
