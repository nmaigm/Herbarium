Alhagi graecorum Boiss.
Diagn. Pl. Orient. 9: 114 (1849).

Synonyms:
Alhagi mannifera Jaub. & Spach, III. Pl. Orient. 5: 1 (1853), nom. illeg.

Morphology:
Perennial herb 30-60 cm, glabrous or sparingly hairy, woody at the base; stems branched, grooved, the branchlets spiny; stipules 1-2 mm, caducous; leaves 0.5-2.5 x 0.2-0.8 cm, oblanceolate, the apex obtuse, truncate or retuse-mucronate; petiole 1-2 mm; inflorescence 1-3-flowered; rhachis terminating in a sharp spine c. 1 cm; bracts c. 1 mm, triangular, persistent; pedicels 1-2 mm; calyx 3-4 mm, the teeth minute; corolla 1-1.2 cm, crimson; pod 2-3.5 x 0.3 cm, terete or slightly compressed, irregularly constricted, _ glabrous or finely strigose; seeds 3 x 2 mm, reniform, brownish.
