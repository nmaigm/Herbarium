Scorpiurus muricatus L.
Sp. Pl., ec. 1, 745 (1753).

Scorpiurus muricatus subsɔ. laevigatus (Sm.) Thell., Mém. Soc. Sci. Nat. Cherbourg 38: 333 (1912).
Scorpiurus muricatus subsɔ. subvillosus (L.) Thell., Mém. Soc. Sci. Nat. Cherbourg 38: 339 (1912).
Scorpiurus muricatus subs. sulcatus (L.) Thell., Mém. Soc. Sci. Nat. Cherbourg 38; 339 (1912).

Morphology:
Annual herb 10-35 cm, appressed-hai-y; stems branched from the base and above; stipules 1.5-2.5 cm, lanceolate-subulate, entire, membranous except the green midrib; leaves 5-15 x 1-2 cm, oblanceolate, with 3-5 main veins, pseudopetiolate, acute; peduncles 5-18 cm; inflorescence umbellate 1 to 3(-5)-flowered; bracts small, scarious; calyx 4 mm, the teeth equalling or slightly shorter than the tube; corolla yellow or reddish; pod 3-5 cm, circinate or contorted, with conspicuous longitudinal ribs, the sutures undulate, constricted between the seeds; seeds c. 3.5 x 2 mm, reniform to falcate, dark brown to blackish, rugulose.
