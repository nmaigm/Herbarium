Reaumuria hirtella Jaub. & Spach
III. Pl. Oriental. 3: 54, 55, t. 244 (1848).

Morphology:
Low shrub 15-40 cm, hirsute or puberulent to glabrescent; stems many, branched, erect or ascending; leaves 0.3-2 x 0.1-0.2 cm, semiterete or flattened, linear, obtuse; bracts many, leaf-like, shorter to longer than the sepals, obtuse, hairy; flowers 0.8-1.5 cm diam., short-pedicellate in interrupted spikes; sepals 0.6-1 cm, connate at the base, the lobes triangular-lanceolate, the margin irregularly denticulate; petals 0.8-1.2 cm, whitish to pinkish, the scales ciliate; filaments dilated and undulate-denticulate at the base; capsule 4-6 mm, ellipsoid-conical; seeds c. 2 mm, with a long hairy tuft.
