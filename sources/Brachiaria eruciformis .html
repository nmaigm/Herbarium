Brachiaria eruciformis (Sm.) Griseb. in Ledeb.
Fl. Ross. 4: 469 (1853).

Synonyms:
Panicum eruciforme Sm. in Sibth. & Sm., Fl. Graec. 1: 44, 1. 59 (1806).
Panicum isachne Roth ex Roem. & Schult., Syst. Veg. 2: 458 (1817)
Brachiaria isachne (Roth ex Roem. & Schult.) Stapf in Prain, Fl. Trop. Afr. 9: 552 (1919).

Morphology:
Annual to 60 cm, slender, geniculately ascending; leaf-blades linear to narrowly lanceolate, glabrous or pubescent; inflorescence 1-8 cm, of 3-14 racemes on an axis; racemes 0.5-2.5 cm, secund, bearing single spikelets imbricate on a triquetrous rhachis; spikelets 1.7-2.7 mm, elliptic, pubescent, subacute, without a stipe; lower glume to 1/5 the length of the spikelet, obtuse; upper glume adjacent to the lower; upper lemma readily deciduous, smooth and shining, obtuse.
