Lantana camara L.
Sp. Pl., ed. 1, 627 (1753).

Synonyms:
Lantana aculeata L., Sp. Pl., ed. 1, 627 (1753).
Lantana antidotalis Schumach. & Thonn., Beskr. Guin. Pl. 276 (1827).
Lantana camara L. var. aculeata (L.) Mold., Torreya 34:9 (1934).

Morphology:
Aromatic shrub 0.4-1.5 m; stems spreading, ± square in section, covered with recurved prickles; all leaves opposite, 2-8 x 2-5 cm, ovate, scabrid-rugose with course tubercle-based hairs, acute, crenate-serrate, the base subcordate; petiole 0.8-1.8 cm; flowers in axillary heads 2.5-4 cm diam.; peduncle 1.5-6 cm; bracts 4-8 x 1-2 mm; calyx 3-4 mm; corolla 0.8-1.2 cm, red, purple, pink, orange or yellow; corolla-tube densely puberulent outside; limb 4-8 mm, the lobes rounded; drupes 4-5.5 mm diam., fleshy, dark purple to black; pyrene c. 3.5 mm diam.
