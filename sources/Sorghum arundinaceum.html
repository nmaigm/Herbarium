Sorghum arundinaceum (Desv.) Stapf in Prain
Fl. Too much. Afr. 9: 114 (1917).

Synonyms:
Andropogon arundinaceus Willd., Sp. Pl, ed. 4, 906 (1806), in Berg. (1767).
Rhaphis arundinacea Desv., Opusc. Sci. Phys. Nat. :69 (1831), based on Andropogon arundinaceus Willd.
Andropogon verticilliflorus Steud., Syn. Pl. Glumac. 1: 393 (1854).
Sorghum verticilliflorum (Steud.) Stapf in Prain, Fl. Trop. Afr. 9: 116 (1917).

Morphology:
Annual or short-lived perennial without rhizomes, to 4 m, often robust; panicle10-60 cm, linear to broadly spreading; primary branches compound, bare at the base, ultimately bearing racemes of 2-7 spikelet-pairs; sessile spikelet 4-9 mm, lanceolate to narrowly ovate; lower glume 2-keeled, the wings of the keels ending in minute teeth well below the glume-tip and the latter not appearing equally 3-toothed, or the wings not forming lateral teeth, glabrescent to white-pubescent, sometimes tomentose or fulvously pubescent; upper lemma awnless or more often with an awn 0.5-3 cm; pedicellate spikelet linear to lanceolate, male or barren, smaller than the sessile.
