Fagonia arabica L. var. arabica
Sp. Pl., ed. 1, 386 (1753).

Morphology:
Low shrub 20-60 cm, entirely covered with sessile or stalked capitate glands, often with adhering sand grains; stems terete, striate; stipular spines to 4 cm; lower leaves 3-foliolate, the upper 1-foliolate or 3-foliolate; leaflets 0.3-1.2(-1.5) cm, narrowly-elliptic to linear, mucronate; pedicel 4-8 mm; flowers 1-1.5 cm diam. at anthesis; sepals 3-4 x 1-1.5 mm, broadly lanceolate, acute, glandular, caducous; petals 7-9 mm, cław 2-2.5 mm, pinkish-violet or mauve; filaments 0.8-1 cm; capsule 4-5 x 5-7 mm, pubescent and glandular; fruit pedicel deflexed; seeds 3 x 2 mm, broadly ovate, tuberculate, with a narrow margin. Plant densely covered with capitate glands and adherent sand grains; lower and upper leaves 3-foliolate, the uppermost 1-foliolate; leaflets 3-5 mm; capsule 5-6 mm diam., hairy and glandular.
