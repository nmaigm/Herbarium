Melica persica Kunth
Revis. Gramin. 1: 122, 351, t. 89 (1830).

Morphology:
Rhizomatous plant to 50 cm, forming dense tufts to 45 cm across; leaf-blades 5-15 x 0.2-0.4 cm, these and the sheaths glabrous or densely pubescent; panicle 3.5-12 cm, spike-like, few-branched and secund to many-branched and cylindrical; spikelets 0.55-1.1 cm, gaping, with 1-2 fertile florets; lower glume 2.5-6.5 mm, broadly ovate, acute; upper glume lanceolate, as long as the spikelet, acute; first fertile lemma 4-7.5 mm, elliptic, strongly 7- to 9-nerved, pilose on the back with tubercle-based hairs 4-5 mm, acute at the tip; second fertile lemma (where present) shorter than the first, glabrous.
