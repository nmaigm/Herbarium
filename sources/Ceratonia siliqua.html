Ceratonia siliqua L., Sp. Pl.
ed. 1, 1026 (1753).

Morphology:
Evergreen tree 2-6 m; leaves 10-25 cm; leaflets 2-4 pairs, 3-6 x 2.5-4 cm, coriaceous, glabrous, short-petioliolate, ovate to oblong-ovate, emarginate or obtuse; racemes solitary or in groups, many flowered; female flowers with a thick deflexed ovary; style short, stigma thick; male flowers with 5 stamens; pod 8-25 x 1.5-2.5 cm, c. 5 mm thick, linear, straight or slightly curved, violet-brown, glossy, mesocarp pulpy, sweet; seeds 5-6 x 4 mm, compressed, obovate, hard, glossy.
