Volutaria lippii (L.) Cass.
ex Maire in Jahand. & Maire, Cat. Pl. Maroc 3: 817 (1934)

Synonyms:
Centaurea lippii L., Sp. Pl., ed. 1, 910 (1753).
Amberboa lippii (L.) DC., Prodr. 6: 559 (1838).

Morphology:
Scabrid annual 20-50 cm; stems branched, grooved; leaves 3-12 x 1-4 cm, pinnatisect. lobes remotely serrate, mucronate; basal leaves petiolate, the cauline sessile and decurrent; capitula terminal, solitary or paired; involucre c. 1.2 x 1 cm, broadly ovoid: phyllaries 0.25-1 x 0.1-0.2 cm, multiseriate, pubescent, with scarious or yellowish margins and a spiny acute yellowish tip, the outer shortest, the middle longer, the innermost longest; corollas 1.5 cm, blue, turning pink when dry; achenes 2.5-2.75 x 1 mm, hairy, hilum lateral; pappus 1.5-2 mm, of white scales.
